# CAD v1.7 — Secondary Reference Audit

## Source class

The newly supplied Etsy/render images are classified as **SECONDARY CAD / RECONSTRUCTION EVIDENCE**.

They are useful for:
- layer ordering;
- clevis/fork construction;
- identification of paired cheek plates belonging to one kinematic body;
- adjustable overlapping link construction;
- qualitative motion-state comparison;
- separation of structural cover/shell parts from the core linkage.

They are **not** used as authoritative original X-Finger manufacturing dimensions.

A current Etsy marketplace search exposes a digital product titled approximately
“Laser Cut Metal Hand Prosthesis: DIY 3D-Printed Finger Segments (Digital File)”.
This supports treating the images as a derivative/commercial CAD reconstruction rather than primary X-Finger documentation.

## Corrections triggered by the new images

### 1. Proximal cam/slot hypothesis downgraded

The previous KIN/CAD v1.5 interpretation of the proximal relation as a cam / pin-in-slot pair is now:

**STATUS: LOW / NOT SUPPORTED**

The new oblique and side views show a predominantly clevis/revolute stacked construction and do not provide convincing evidence of a true operational sliding cam pair.

The primary patent model remains based on pivoted links unless direct evidence of a slot-guided DOF is recovered.

### 2. Paired cheeks are not automatically separate kinematic bodies

Several doubled side plates in the new renders are fork/clevis cheeks surrounding a central member.
They may be physically separate pieces but function as one rigid body when clamped/fastened together.

CAD body assignment must therefore distinguish:
- physical part count;
- kinematic rigid-body count.

### 3. Adjustable bars

Upper and lower perforated members clearly show:
- repeated adjustment holes;
- overlapping sections;
- paired locking screws.

These are fitting/size-adjustment features, not operational DOFs after tightening.

### 4. Distal carrier

The distal joint is a stacked sandwich of:
- carrier/side plates;
- central link(s);
- through pivots;
- external tip-cover pieces.

The amber cover geometry is treated as shell/cover unless a pivot is shown to transmit load into the core linkage.

## Current design policy

Primary evidence priority:
1. Didrick patent.
2. User-supplied original photographs/videos.
3. User-reconstructed 2D profiles and multi-pose overlays.
4. Secondary commercial CAD renders such as these Etsy images.

Secondary renders may confirm architecture, but cannot override contradictory primary evidence without independent support.
